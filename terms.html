1. What this software is

"Filosofia e IA — Publisher" (the "Tool") is a private, single-creator publishing tool. It uploads short educational videos — produced by the operator's own content pipeline — to the operator's own social media accounts, including the TikTok account @prof.edson.almeida, and reads back the performance of those same videos so the operator can decide what to produce next.

2. Who may use it

The Tool is used exclusively by the operator, on accounts owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public, is not sold, sublicensed or resold, and does not accept registrations from third parties. No other person is granted access to it.

3. What the Tool does and does not do

The Tool:

  • authenticates against TikTok using the official Content Posting API and TikTok API for Business,

with the account holder's explicit authorization;

  • uploads video files the operator produced, together with their captions;
  • discloses AI-generated content using TikTok's own disclosure flag whenever the video was produced

with generative AI;

  • reads metrics of the operator's own published videos.

The Tool does not:

  • post on behalf of any account other than the operator's own;
  • collect, store or process data belonging to other TikTok users;
  • scrape TikTok, automate engagement (likes, follows, comments), or attempt to influence rankings by

artificial means;

  • publish content that violates TikTok's Community Guidelines or Terms of Service.
