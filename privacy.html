Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Controller: Edson Almeida, individual creator, Brazil · Contact: filosofoedson@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy covers "Filosofia e IA — Publisher" (the "Tool"), a private publishing tool used by one creator on the creator's own social media accounts, including the TikTok account @prof.edson.almeida.

The Tool has no users other than its operator. It does not offer sign-up, does not host accounts and does not process personal data of third parties.

2. What data the Tool accesses

With the account holder's explicit authorization, and only for the operator's own account:

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Basic account info (open id, display name, avatar)to identify the authorized account before publishingaccess token stored as an environment variable in the operator's private server
Video metadata of the operator's own posts (id, caption, publish date)to match a published video with the internal production recordSupabase database, private, owned by the operator
Performance metrics of the operator's own posts (views, reach, watch time, completion rate, likes, comments, shares, impression sources, audience countries in aggregate form)to decide which topics and formats to produce nextsame database

The Tool never accesses data about other TikTok users, their profiles, their content or their private information. Audience data is only ever handled in the aggregate form the platform itself returns (for example, "share of views by country"), never at the level of an identifiable person.

3. How the data is stored and protected

  • Data lives in a private Supabase (PostgreSQL) project and in a private n8n instance, both accessible only to the operator, over TLS.
  • Access tokens and API secrets are stored as environment variables, never in source code, and are rotated when there is any suspicion of exposure.
  • Nothing is shared with, sold to, or transferred to third parties. There is no advertising, no profiling of other people, and no data broker involved.

4. Retention

Metrics of the operator's own posts are retained while the channel is active, because the whole point of collecting them is to compare performance over time. Access tokens are deleted when authorization is revoked. Any data can be deleted on request to the contact address above.

5. Your rights

Because the only person whose data is processed is the operator, there is no third-party data subject. Even so, any question about this policy can be sent to filosofoedson@gmail.com and will be answered.

6. Revoking access

Authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Revocation immediately stops all access by the Tool.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
