Novelas de Natal

Novelas de Natal ("Christmas Novellas") is a private automation tool operated by Edson Almeida, an individual creator and teacher based in Brazil. It produces Christmas short stories and publishes them to the operator's own accounts.

What the app does

  • Writes, formats and organizes Christmas short stories produced by the operator.
  • Uses Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0) so the operator can authorize the tool to work with the operator's own Google account, for example to save files or publish the operator's own content.
  • It has no public sign-up and no users other than its operator.
